# 06 · Writing Guidelines

- **Plain and specific.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype, no urgency,
  no "secrets", "hacks", or "the one weird trick".
- **Explain sequence and trade-off**, not prescription. The reader is capable;
  they need the map, not marching orders.
- **Active voice.** "Convert in low-income years", not "conversions should be
  considered".
- **Define on first use**, then move on. Assume intelligence, not expertise.
- **One idea per paragraph.** If a paragraph has two, split it.
- **Numbers stay verbatim** in extracted content (see
  [05_finance_rules](05_finance_rules.md)).
- **No filler.** Cut "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note",
  and every sentence that could be deleted without loss.
