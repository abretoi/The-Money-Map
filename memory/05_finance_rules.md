# 05 · Finance Rules

This is educational content, not advice. Guardrails:

1. **Never invent figures.** Every number in an `extracted` block comes from the
   source infographic, verbatim. No rounding, no "updating", no conversion.
2. **Stamp dated figures.** Contribution limits, benefit maximums, tax brackets,
   median prices carry `asOf` so they can be flagged as they age. Most source
   figures are 2025–2026.
3. **Preserve disclaimers.** Every map's advisor disclaimer is retained as a
   `note` block with `variant: disclaimer`. Never drop it.
4. **No personalized advice.** Lessons explain trade-offs and sequencing. They
   do not tell an individual what to do with their money.
5. **Calculators (Milestone 4) compute, they do not recommend.** Show the math;
   let the reader draw the conclusion.

When a figure looks wrong, do not "correct" it in an extracted block — that
would fabricate a source. Flag it for human review instead.
