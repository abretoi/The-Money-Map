Most net worth advice is a single number to chase. This map replaces that with
a ladder: seven bands, each carrying one highest-value move instead of a
sprawling to-do list. The move at $50k is not the move at $1M, and pretending
otherwise wastes effort at every rung.

The middle section reframes the same climb as a shift in posture. Early on,
the job is growth and staying invested through volatility. In the middle, the
job becomes balancing pre-tax, Roth, and taxable money while adding real
protection. Later, the job becomes preservation, giving, and coordination
across a lifetime of decisions.

The five mistakes are not rare — they are the default failure modes at nearly
every band, from lifestyle inflation right after a raise to an estate plan
nobody has touched since a decade-old marriage.
