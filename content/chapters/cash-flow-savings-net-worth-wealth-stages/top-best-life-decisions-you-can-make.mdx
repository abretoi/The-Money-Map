Most advice about building wealth focuses on accounts and allocations. This
map makes a different claim: the highest-leverage decisions in a financial
life are not financial at all — they are decisions about partner, health,
skills, environment, and habits.

The scoreboard is blunt about it. Marrying well, prioritizing health, and
saving consistently all score a "Massive" lifetime payoff — the same tier as
building career capital, which is the more conventional financial advice.
Decisions like setting boundaries or moving away from toxic environments score
lower only in relative terms; they are still rated "High."

The Life Decision Filter and Core Principles sections exist to operationalize
this. Instead of asking "can I afford this," the filter asks whether a
decision aligns with values, holds up in ten years, and creates optionality
rather than dependence — a values-first lens applied to choices well beyond
spending.
