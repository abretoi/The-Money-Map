This map takes a single reference household — U.S., age 35-44 proxy — and
lays its entire annual budget out in one view: $127,388/yr coming in,
$91,229/yr going out, and a $36,159/yr gap between the two.

The seven-bucket breakdown makes the shape of that spending obvious. Housing
alone is $30,480/yr, more than double the next-largest bucket. Transportation
and food follow, then insurance and pensions, then healthcare, then the
smaller entertainment and misc buckets.

The "What the Blueprint Says" table regroups those seven buckets into four
functional categories — core living costs, wealth and protection, lifestyle
and misc, and the gap itself — which is a more useful way to reason about
tradeoffs than looking at any single line item alone.
