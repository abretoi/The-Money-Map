Most investors think about *what* to invest in. This map is about *when* taxes
get paid on that investment — a decision that compounds just as much as
returns do.

The three buckets sit on different timelines. Tax-deferred accounts (Traditional
401(k), Traditional IRA) trade a deduction now for taxation later. Tax-free
accounts (Roth 401(k), Roth IRA, HSA for medical use) skip the deduction but
never tax growth or withdrawal. Taxable brokerage accounts get no upfront
break and carry an annual tax drag, but offer full access and favorable
long-term capital gains treatment.

The 2025 rate structure underneath all of this — ordinary brackets from 10%
to 37%, long-term capital gains at 0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income — is
what makes the bucket choice matter. The map's practical answer for a
household in their 30s is not to pick one bucket, but to use all three, since
each is built for a different tax future.
