actionSteps:
  - Compute 28% of your own gross monthly income and treat it as a ceiling.

The list price is the number everyone quotes. It is not the number that decides
whether a house is affordable.

Across the three down-payment scenarios below, the home is *the same house* at
the same price. What changes is the cash required at closing and the resulting
monthly payment — and the two move in opposite directions. Going from 3.5% down
to 20% down saves roughly $418 a month, but demands about $68,000 more up front.

The section that matters most is the one titled *What the Payment Misses*. Every
payment figure on this map is principal and interest only. Property taxes,
homeowners insurance, PMI, HOA dues, and maintenance are all additional, and
together they routinely add 25–40% on top of the quoted payment.
