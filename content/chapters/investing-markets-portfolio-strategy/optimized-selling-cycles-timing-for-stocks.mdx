Selling is the half of investing that gets the least structure. Buy
decisions get research, theses, and valuation models; sell decisions too
often get a gut feeling on a red day. This map inverts that by giving selling
the same staged treatment as buying.

The Selling Ladder is the core mechanism: partial trim, rebalance, hold past
one year for tax treatment, exit fully, harvest losses — a graduated response
that matches the severity of the reaction to the severity of the signal. The
Sell Signal Decision Matrix translates six concrete situations, from a
euphoric price spike to a personal liquidity need, into a specific action.

Running underneath all of it is a tax layer that most retail decisions skip:
holding past a year for long-term rates, offsetting gains with losses up to
the $3,000 limit, and spreading sales across tax years to avoid bunching
income. The map's closing line makes the real goal explicit — not a perfect
exit, but a repeatable process.
