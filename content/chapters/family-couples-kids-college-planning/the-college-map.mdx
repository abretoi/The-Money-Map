College cost conversations tend to start and end at the sticker price. This
map splits the decision into four separate questions: what will it actually
cost, how should it be paid for, what's the return on each path, and how do
you keep making good decisions once you're enrolled.

The Cost of Attendance Breakdown shows why "in-state vs. out-of-state vs.
private" is not a rounding error — the gap between $29,400 and $63,700 a year
compounds fast over four years. The Funding Stack answers the payment
question with an explicit order: free money first, tax-advantaged savings
next, cash flow and work-study before any borrowing, and federal loans ahead
of private loans if borrowing is unavoidable.

The ROI table reframes "which school" as "which path," comparing four
distinct routes — flagship public, private, honors college, and community
college transfer — on trade-offs rather than prestige alone. The Smart
College Playbook and Planning Bands close the loop: net price, completion
rates, and debt discipline matter more than the name on the diploma.
