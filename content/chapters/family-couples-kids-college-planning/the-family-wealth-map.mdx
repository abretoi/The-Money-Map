Family wealth planning tends to get postponed because it feels like it
requires legal expertise before anything can be done. This map breaks that
into a stack of concrete, sequential layers instead.

The Family Protection Stack starts with the basics — an emergency fund and
term life insurance — before layering in disability coverage, umbrella
liability protection, and an annual beneficiary review. The Core Estate
Documents table then names the five specific documents that stack rests on,
each with a distinct legal purpose. Kids & Wealth Tools and Gifting & Legacy
Basics extend the same logic to the next generation, from 529 plans to the
$19,000 annual gift exclusion.

The Family Wealth Checklist is the map's most useful tool for revisiting this
periodically — it lines up priorities, documents, and what "success" looks
like across five life stages, from a New Couple building the basics to a
Legacy Stage focused on succession and family governance.
