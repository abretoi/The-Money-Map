Most people think about income as a single number on a paycheck. This map
treats it as a ladder, with labor income at the bottom — trading time for
money — and royalty or passive income at the top, where earnings continue
with little active involvement.

The ratings table is the interesting part: it does not just list ten income
streams, it scores each one on scalability and resilience. Salary is
resilient but barely scalable. Dividends and digital products score well on
both, which is exactly why the closing line argues for a mix rather than a
single source.

The life-stage section and the Smart Income Mix at the bottom turn the ladder
into a sequence: build a reliable base, add one side stream, convert surplus
into assets, then layer in ownership and portfolio income as time allows.
