This map treats income as something you can actively design rather than
passively receive. The five sections build a case for earning power as an
asset in its own right, alongside more familiar assets like savings and
investments.

The Big Idea frames modern work as flexible and ownership-oriented — skills
compound, and building brands or systems creates leverage that a paycheck
alone doesn't. The Key Learnings turn that into eight practical mindsets, and
the Practical Playbook sequences a path from "strengthen one skill" through
to "turn income gains into savings and investments."

The Common Money Traps section is a caution against two opposite failure
modes: waiting too long for perfect clarity, and — once income starts
growing — spending it instead of investing it. The takeaways compress the
whole map into five short commitments centered on skills, value, ownership,
and continued experimentation.
