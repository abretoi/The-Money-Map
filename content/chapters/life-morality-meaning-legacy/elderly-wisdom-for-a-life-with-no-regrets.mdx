This map is an outlier in the Money Map series: there isn't a dollar figure
anywhere on it. It's still squarely about financial planning, though, because
it answers the question every other map assumes but never states outright —
*what is all this money actually for?*

The wisdom table is the structural core. Each row pairs a mistake most people
recognize in themselves — chasing image, constant worry, delaying joy,
neglecting friendship, overworking, holding grudges — with the perspective
elders arrive at once the stakes of those mistakes become clear in
hindsight.

The Wisdom Practice turns that hindsight into something usable now: laugh
more, schedule people instead of just tasks, spend less energy on comparison,
say yes to adventures, and let something larger than logistics anchor your
life. None of it requires waiting until later to start.
