Not every map in this series runs on dollar figures. This one is a values
checklist — twenty-five short principles sorted into five domains: character,
relationships, growth, money and work, and inner peace.

Money and work get only one of the five sections, and even there the
principles are about behavior, not numbers: live below your means, invest in
your skills, avoid ego spending, build assets rather than just income, and
treat time as the scarce resource it is.

Read the five sections as reinforcing each other rather than ranked. The
closing line — integrity, stewardship, gratitude, and service — is presented
as the foundation the other twenty-one principles stand on.
