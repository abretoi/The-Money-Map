Jean Chatzky and Kathryn Tuggle's *How to Money* is built around a reassuring
premise: you don't need financial jargon to be good with money. The five big
ideas — clarity, consistent habits, plain language, confidence through action,
and foundations before optimization — set the tone for everything below them.

The eight key learnings and the five-step practical playbook both point the
same direction: budget first, automate saving, keep credit habits clean,
invest consistently, and review regularly. It's a sequence, not a menu — each
step assumes the one before it is already in place.

The common money traps section is a useful mirror. Overcomplicating a plan,
spending without a plan, letting small debts compound, and avoiding money
because it feels stressful are four different ways people stall out before
the playbook even gets a chance to work.
