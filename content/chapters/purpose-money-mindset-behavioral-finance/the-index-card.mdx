Most personal finance advice tries to sound sophisticated. This map argues the
opposite: a handful of rules, followed consistently, cover most of what
matters. The Big Idea section states it directly — protection and debt control
matter, but so does simply not overcomplicating things.

The Key Learnings and the Practical Playbook are two views of the same
material. The Key Learnings are the *what* — eight independent habits with no
required order. The Playbook is the *when* — a five-step sequence that starts
with eliminating high-interest debt and ends with reviewing insurance and
estate basics.

The Common Money Traps section is the mirror image of the rules: each trap is
what happens when a rule gets ignored. Overcomplicating investing is the
opposite of low-cost index investing; underinsuring major risks is the
opposite of buying adequate insurance.
