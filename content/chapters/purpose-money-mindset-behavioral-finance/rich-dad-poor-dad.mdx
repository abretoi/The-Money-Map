*Rich Dad Poor Dad* is rarely read for its technical precision — it endures
because of the mental model it installs. This map treats it that way: not as
a set of literal instructions, but as a framework for noticing the difference
between what builds wealth and what merely looks like it.

The five sections trace a path from belief to behavior. The Big Idea reframes
assets, liabilities, and cash flow. The Key Learnings turn that reframe into
eight habits of thought. The Practical Playbook converts those habits into a
five-step sequence you can actually follow, in order. The Common Money Traps
section is the map's own caution against misreading the book too literally.

Read the Practical Money Map Takeaways last — they compress everything above
into five short commitments you can revisit without rereading the whole page.
