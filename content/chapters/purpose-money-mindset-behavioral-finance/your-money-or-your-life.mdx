Vicki Robin and Joe Dominguez's central move is a unit conversion: instead of
pricing things in dollars, price them in hours of your life. A $60 dinner
stops being $60 and becomes however many hours of work — after taxes and
commuting — it actually cost you. That reframing is the engine behind
everything else on this map.

The eight Key Learnings build a tracking discipline first, then a values
filter second. You cannot separate fulfillment from impulse buying, or spot
lifestyle drift, until you can see where the money — and the life energy —
actually goes. The five-step playbook turns that discipline into a repeatable
loop: measure, convert, cut, invest.

The traps section names the quiet failure mode: income rising while spending
rises to match it, so the gap that produces freedom never opens up. The fix
isn't more income. It's a clearer definition of enough.
