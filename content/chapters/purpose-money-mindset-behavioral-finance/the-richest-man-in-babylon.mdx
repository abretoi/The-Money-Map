Clason wrapped his advice in parables set in ancient Babylon, but the rules
underneath are mechanical, not mystical. Keep part of what you earn. Put it to
work. Protect it. Repeat, for a long time.

The five-step playbook on this map is deliberately sequential: save first,
then sort needs from wants, then build a reserve, then invest, then keep
learning to raise your earning power. Each step depends on the one before it —
you cannot safely invest what you have not first protected with a reserve.

The four traps are really one trap wearing different masks: acting before
understanding. Lifestyle inflation, chasing hot tips, lending without
diligence, and procrastination all trade a known present cost for an unknown
future one.
