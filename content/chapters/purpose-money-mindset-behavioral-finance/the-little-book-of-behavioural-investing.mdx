James Montier's central claim is uncomfortable: the biggest threat to your
portfolio is usually not the market — it is you. Overconfidence, herd
behavior, and short-termism show up again and again as the drivers of poor
outcomes, and none of them require a market crash to do damage.

The map's answer is process over prediction. A written plan for diversifying,
anchoring to valuation, and rebalancing on schedule removes the moment-to-moment
decisions where bias creeps in. The five-step playbook is deliberately boring —
that is the point.

The closing takeaways are worth reading as a checklist before any trade:
biases cost money, crowds can mislead, and discipline is the edge that is
actually available to an ordinary investor.
