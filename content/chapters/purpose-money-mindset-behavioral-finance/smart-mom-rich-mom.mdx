Family finance rarely fails because of one bad decision. It erodes through
small, unexamined gaps — a paused contribution here, a missed insurance
review there — that compound the same way savings do, just in reverse. This
map's core claim is that consistency beats perfection.

The five sections build outward from that idea: the Big Idea frames why
family money needs deliberate planning, the Key Learnings turn that into
eight specific postures, and the Practical Playbook sequences them into five
concrete moves, starting with knowing your actual cash-flow numbers.

Two threads run through every section: protecting earning power (skills,
health, career growth) gets the same weight as protecting savings, and
automation is the mechanism that makes both survive a busy family schedule
without relying on constant attention.
