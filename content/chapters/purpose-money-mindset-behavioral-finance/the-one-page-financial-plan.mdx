Most financial plans fail from complexity, not from bad math. This map argues
the opposite approach: start with what matters to you, write it down, and
build the simplest system that moves you toward it.

The five-step playbook treats planning as a sequence — priorities first, then
goals with timeframes, then a savings system, then spending alignment, and
finally an annual review. Each step depends on the one before it; a savings
system without stated priorities has nothing to aim at.

The "Common Money Traps" section is a warning against two opposite failure
modes: drifting with no written plan at all, and obsessively optimizing a plan
that has lost touch with what it was supposed to serve. The map's answer to
both is the same — write it down, keep it simple, and revisit it calmly.
