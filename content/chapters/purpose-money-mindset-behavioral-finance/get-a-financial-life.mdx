Most financial advice starts with investing. This map starts earlier, with the
habits that make investing possible in the first place: knowing your cash
flow, using credit deliberately, and starting to save before the amounts feel
meaningful.

The five-step playbook is the spine of the map. It is deliberately ordered —
open the right accounts, track cash flow, build a starter reserve, clear
high-interest debt, then automate the rest. Skipping ahead to automation
before the reserve exists just means the first emergency undoes the plan.

The "Common Money Traps" section is worth sitting with. None of the four traps
listed are about complex financial instruments — they are about paycheck
psychology, fine print, timing, and procrastination. That is consistent with
the map's closing line: the goal is control, not complexity.
