Most money advice assumes the obstacle is information — the right budget, the
right account, the right rate. This map argues the real obstacle is often
older and less visible: a fear response that has been running the show since
before the numbers ever came into it.

The four traps below aren't character flaws. Avoidance, emotional spending,
fear-based hoarding, and shame-driven secrecy are all coping strategies that
made sense at some point and now cost more than they protect. Naming them is
what starts to loosen their grip.

The playbook that follows isn't a five-step fix. It's a loop — name the fear,
audit honestly, build routine, build buffer, review with compassion — meant to
run again and again, each time with a little more safety in place than the
time before.
