A strong financial life requires defense as much as offense. These maps focus on the systems that protect cash flow and optionality: emergency funds, credit health, debt payoff order, and resilience against life disruptions. The goal is not just to eliminate bad debt, but to build a stable base that makes every other financial decision easier.
